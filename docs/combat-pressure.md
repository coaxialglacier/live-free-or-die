# Live Free or Die — Combat Pressure by Place

Working design for what happens when the player camps. Stillness always feeds the Crown. The punishment must belong to the place.

Related: [`../Live-Free-Or-Die-Concept.md`](../Live-Free-Or-Die-Concept.md) · [`combat.md`](combat.md) · [`enemies.md`](enemies.md)

---

## Law

**Camping always gets punished. The punishment must belong to the place.**

Town can grow ranks and wheel up a gun. A forest bite, a night raid, a lonely road — those cannot magically spawn a regiment and a cannon without feeling fake.

Same design job (make passivity lose). Different monster.

If every fight becomes “wait too long → reinforcements → artillery,” players stop reading the world and start reading a reused meter.

---

## Shared spine: Settle Meter

Every encounter type can have a quiet **Settle Meter**:

- You go still / safe / uncurious → meter rises
- You break them, relocate, breach, make noise with purpose → meter drops or resets

What the meter *buys the enemy* changes by fantasy.

Player-facing teaches should be world lines, not tutorial dumps:

- Town: “They’re dressing the line.”
- Forest: “They’re moving on the trails.”
- Raid: “The house is waking.”
- Patrol: “One’s breaking for the road.”

Different sentence, same gut: *move or lose the shape of this fight.*

## Town / garrison street — the machine

Sit still and the map turns British.

| Stage | What you feel | What’s happening |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| **1. Dressing the line** | Shots get closer; peek windows shrink | They stop panicking, start sharing angles |
| **2. Volley ownership** | Leaving cover feels heavier; movement gets muddy under fire | Coordinated fire pins you |
| **3. Enfilade** | Flanks light up; favorite wall becomes a trap | Light Bobs / Loyalists wrap while the line holds your face |
| **4. Artillery invitation** | Drum / rider / Gorget shout | They’re calling the gun |
| **5. Cannon / Grasshopper setup** | A timer you can hear | Crew wheels / unlimbers / loads; cover is provisional |
| **6. Obliteration** | Wall, cart, chimney, tavern corner — gone | Not chip damage. A statement. Your camp is a crater |

### Harder to move — readable, not cheap

When the line owns the street:

- Sprint out of cover and the air fills with warning cracks
- Vaults under volley have flinch / stumble risk unless you smoke first
- Long open crossings become stupid on purpose
- Reload still works (never jail the hands); the *space* gets hostile

This is pressure on **positioning**, not a root debuff that steals Doom tempo. You can always run. Running across a dressed line without breaking it should feel braver than it is smart.

Break the line, and mobility snaps back.

### Cover is a loan, not a home

Stone walls, wagons, doorframes, trees, smoke you made — friends until artillery stage:

- Soft cover annihilated
- Hard cover cracked, then deleted
- Smoke still helps for a beat, then the ball arrives anyway

Cover is for **reloading while moving through**, not for filing taxes.

### How you stop the machine

- Kill the Bloodyback / Gorget — dressing stutters; heat drops
- Force a bad volley — bait, smoke, break LOS, crash reload window
- Murder Matrosses mid-setup — highest value breach targets
- Steal / spike the gun — late fantasy punchline
- Keep fracturing them — every breach kill and stolen musket delays stage-up

Aggression is the extinguisher. Passivity is the invitation.

### Audio telegraph (never cheap)

- Stage 1: sharper ordered shouting
- Stage 2: volley rhythm gets metronome-clean
- Stage 3: whistles on the flanks
- Stage 4: rider / drum / “Bring up the gun!”
- Stage 5: wheels, chain, crew calls, a long awful patience
- Stage 6: the meanest boom in the game — then a hole where your plan was

If cover dies with no warning, it’s grief.  
If cover dies because you heard the whole argument and still sat there, it’s comedy.

**Line infantry are a snowball. You are the shovel.**

## Threat kits by place

### Forest / wilderness ambush — the land turns

No endless Lobsterback printer. No cannon.

If you hunker after first contact goes loud:

- Beaters and dogs push from the blind side
- Skirmishers / Jägers uphill take the tree that was “safe”
- Your smoke becomes a marker — they surround the cloud
- Silence predators: a patient marksman who only gets lethal if you stay predictable
- Escape routes close — not with walls, with men on the game trails you meant to use

Worst camp ending: you are not bombarded; you are **pocketed**. The forest that was your ally becomes a bag.

Kalha upside: if *you* keep moving, the same land pockets *them*.

Butler’s Rangers, Jägers, Mohawk/Seneca war parties own this grammar when the story places them here.

### Farm / stone-wall country (Lexington road energy) — the field gets measured

Reinforcements can exist, smaller and later. Cannon only if a real road column is nearby.

Camping threat instead:

- They stop charging stupid and start **counting fences**
- Crossfires at lane corners
- A flanking file that makes your wall face the wrong way
- Livestock / civilians panic and ruin sightlines (or Spark)
- Controlled burns / flush tactics on hedges and outbuildings

Worst camp ending: the wall is still standing and somehow useless. You’re framed.

Gallopers matter here: one rider breaking free is tomorrow’s garrison problem.

### Night raid / docks / warehouse — the alarm becomes the boss

Spawning a parade square mid-cellar is dumb. Spawning *consequences* is not.

If you freeze in cover after the first shot:

- Alarm stages climb (bell, horn, shutter lamps, boots on boards)
- Doors you needed lock or fill
- Patrol paths tighten into a net
- A competent knot answers — quality, not quantity (often **Jollies** on the waterfront)
- Soft objective failure: powder moved, papers burned, target flees upstairs

Worst camp ending: you survive the room and **lose the reason you came**.

### Hideout defense / doorway fight — time is the cannon

Threat while you camp a window:

- They breach other doors
- Helpers die / flee (Spark and Belief take the hit)
- Intel burns whether you like it or not (unless they seize it first)
- Fire spreads; your workshop becomes the munition
- One hard entry team — forlorn hope energy, small, nasty, personal
- **Catchpoles** tilt the fight toward custody and the rope

Worst camp ending: you held one doorway perfectly and the home died behind you.

### Small patrol / lonely road (3–8 coats) — competence, not census

No reinforcements. No artillery. Sitting still still fails.

Threat:

- They stop peeking and **commit together**
- One Galloper / runner tries to leave for help (the real bomb is the future)
- They fix bayonets and take your cover at a walk
- They feint open, then club the angle you keep using
- If the runner escapes, *next* map sector gets hotter — deferred punishment

Worst camp ending: you win a slow ugly fight and hear the rider already gone. You didn’t lose the scrap; you **summoned the town machine for later**.

### Siege edge / battlefield collision — orders, weather, weight

Big toys can exist here, but should not copy town 1:1.

Camping threat:

- Barrage lanes shift onto your stagnant grid square
- Friendly militia melt if you don’t make a move (Spark decay)
- Cavalry / Gallopers scrape your backside
- Smoke from *their* guns blinds you into their advance

Worst camp ending: history happens on purpose and you’re furniture in it.

## Contrast card

| Place | If you camp, they get… | Punchline |
|-------|-------------------------|-----------|
| Town | Ranks + Grasshopper / cannon | Cover erased |
| Forest | Encirclement + hunters | Pocketed |
| Farm lanes | Measured crossfire + Gallopers | Wall faces wrong way |
| Night raid | Alarm + objective bleed | Mission dies |
| Hideout door | Multi-breach + helper loss | Home dies |
| Lonely patrol | Competence + runner | Future gets worse |
| Big battle | Shifting weight | You’re scenery |

Same lesson: **stillness feeds the Crown.**  
Different teeth: so each place stays a place.

---

## Design rule against same-same

Before an encounter ships, answer:

1. What can fictionally arrive if I wait?
2. What cannot?
3. What local dread replaces the thing that cannot?

If the answer to 3 is “more Lobsterbacks and a cannon” again, rewrite it.

---

## Pairing with fun combat

Reload-anywhere ([`combat.md`](combat.md)) means your hands never stop.  
Pressure-by-place means your feet are what keep the Crown from unpacking its best tricks.

Town is the industrial nightmare. Forest is the bag. Night is the failed job. Patrol is the messenger you should have caught.
