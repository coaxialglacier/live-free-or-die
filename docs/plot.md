## The Trio

| Character | Working identity | Fighting language |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Tehalkalhaku (“Kalha”)** | Oneida, Wolf Clan | Ambush tempo, tomahawk, reading the land |
| **Colony Dockhand** | Colony-born brawler-marksman | Fists, hidden pistols, crowd grit |
| **British Traitor** | Redcoat-trained, turned Patriot | Saber precision, enemy-read, pistol duels |

Shared fantasy: three brothers in the cause. Switch between them across the campaign.

---

## Plot chain (current focus)

Cause and effect: help Kalha’s people → earn Oneida alliance → later woods ambush succeeds fully. Refuse or fail → redcoats escape with part of the prize.

```
Catawba battle
    → Welcome at Oneida town
    → Council dialogue (join Crown / Colonies / stay neutral)
    → Alliance state set
    → Later: British crate escort in the woods
         ├─ With Oneida help → full wipe, full upgrade loot
         └─ Without → partial win, some escape, weaker upgrade / later pain
```

## Beat 1 — Help the Oneida vs the Catawba

**Setup:** The trio is already together (coming off patriot work). A runner finds Kalha: his people are mustering against a **Catawba** war party / counter-strike.

**Ask:** Not a Congress contract. Kinship.

> “My people go to meet the Catawba at the ford. If you are my brothers, you come.”

**Why the other two say yes**
- Dockhand: street loyalty; does not leave his boy.
- Traitor: wants to; also knows Oneida debt is strategic — and wants to see a real Indigenous meeting fight.

**Mission shape:** Not an ambush (save that for Beat 4).
- Daylight / dusk **meeting fight** at a ford, **or** assault on a palisaded Catawba camp.
- Open commitment, war cries, muskets and steel.

**Roles in the fight**
- Kalha — closes, tomahawk tempo, reads their charge
- Dockhand — breaks bodies, dirty pistol work, holds buckling line
- Traitor — picks leaders / best fighters; cold counters

**Win:** Break the Catawba war party / take the camp.  
**Payoff into Beat 2:** The boys are owed welcome — and a voice — among Kalha’s people.

## Beat 2 — Welcome home

Trio returns with Kalha to the Oneida town (working location: **Kanonwalohale** if timed to 1775 deliberations).

- Food, tobacco, names spoken.
- Marked as men who showed up for Oneida kin, not for pay.
- Sets standing for the council: guests with blood-credit, not recruiters with a pitch.

---

## Beat 3 — Council fire (interactive dialogue)

**Historic hinge:** Oneida deliberations, especially **June 1775 neutrality council**, with pressure continuing through **1776–77** (see lore doc).

**Scene:** Interactive dialogue sequence at council. Sachems, warriors, clan mothers (“female governesses”). Kirkland may sit at the edge. Arguments for neutrality, Crown, and Colonies all get air.

**Player tools:** Tag-team lines by character
- Kalha — kinship, land, what the Catawba fight proved
- Dockhand — Crown never loved the common man; liberty spreads
- Traitor — how the King uses Native allies and discards them; military reality

**Design rule:** Not a single speech check. Choices shift national **Belief / Spark** and set alliance temperature (patriotic smudge allowed on timing/intensity; sovereignty and stakes stay honest).

**Outcomes (working)**
| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Strong, respectful win | Oneida lean Patriot earlier / louder; full later support |
| Partial / cautious | Personal debt to Kalha holds; national help is thinner |
| Botched / colonial arrogance | Cooler nation; later ambush lacks full Oneida strength |

**Closing image (working):** Council fire covered so each nation may walk its own road — and the Oneida choose the road with the trio’s footprints on it.

## Beat 4 — British crates in the woods

**Setup:** Redcoat escort moving mechanical advancements / supply the Crown needs (Cornwallis flavor when act timing fits). Trio hits them in the forest with muskets and tomahawks.

**If Beat 1–3 earned Oneida help**
- Full massacre; nobody rides out.
- All crates taken.
- Flintlock upgrade from captured British mechanical improvements.
- Upstream commander feels the loss (men and materiel).

**If Oneida help is missing / thin**
- Trio still hits hard; line too long.
- Courier detail breaks out with part of the shipment.
- Partial or lesser upgrade.
- Escapees become later pressure (tighter patrols, fortified depot, named officer who got away).

**Combat fantasy:** Woods ambush — vanish, volley, close during reload, finish with steel. Distinct from the Catawba stand-up fight.

## Systems touched by this chain

- **Alliance / debt:** Help peoples who matter to a patriot; they show up later.
- **Belief / Spark:** Council dialogue moves local will, not only quest flags.
- **Loot as theft:** Upgrades come out of Crown crates, not a shop.
- **Loadout as trophy:** Gun grows as a museum of British failures.
- **Patriotic smudge:** Tip timing and boldness of Oneida commitment; do not flip who the fight is for.
