A third-person American Revolutionary War action game. Three patriots. One cause. History that bends toward liberty.

## The Pitch

Pro-American. Action first.

You play the people who make 1776 happen. Experience gritty, cinematic third-person action in colonial streets, forests, docks, and battlefields. The story starts long before the war begins. Liberty is something people risk everything for. The Crown provides real pressure. You are the kind of person who starts the Revolution and wins it.

## The Trio

You control all three characters across the same campaign. Background chooses how your hands work. The war is the sentence you write with them. Switch between them as the colonies catch fire. Sabotage one night, saber work the next, a wilderness ambush after that.

### Oneida Resister

Raised in the world of the Iroquois Confederacy nations that stood against the British. Tomahawk, ambush tempo, and “reading the land” abilities. The forest itself fights with you. Expect woodland flanking, vanish steps, and sudden violence. Break redcoat formations by refusing their ground.

### Colony-Born Dockhand

Scrappy brawler-marksman. Farm muscle, dock scars, and little muskets hidden in his clothes. The ordinary colonist who becomes a legend because he will not leave the street. Fists, rifle butts, and bayonet rushes. Crowd surges happen when you fight hard enough and neighbors join.

### British Traitor

Redcoat-trained, then turned. Precise saber fighting and insight into enemy movements. He knows their drill, their tells, and the exact second a volley line fails. Parry-riposte saber school and controlled pistol duels. He reads British tactics and punishes reloads.

## The Action

Hunt Loyalists, informants, and enemies of the cause through alleys and taverns. Fight redcoat patrols in sudden street scraps and full battlefield collisions. Sabotage powder stores, docks, messengers, and supply lines before dawn. Serve on secret missions for George Washington. Do the quiet jobs that never make the painting.

## Patriotic 

Broadly historical. Freedom-empowered mythos on purpose.

The game stays close enough to history to feel true. Then it lets you shove it. Delay the war or start it early. Push hard enough and the timeline tips from record into American myth. You will see bigger stands, braver rides, cleaner victories, and ideals embodied at full volume.

### Allowed Smudges

Tip a near-loss into a hard-won win. Expose a Loyalist plot earlier. Rally a hesitant town. Get the alarm farther by dawn. Help the right hero arrive in time.

### Locked Truth

America is the righteous fight. The Revolution is worth winning. Smudges make the founding more legendary. They do not flip the moral center of the story.

### Belief and Spark

Local patriotic fervor matters. Win fights but lose the people, and the street goes quiet. Win the people, and militias rise mid-mission.

## Campaign Shape

It starts before the shot heard round the world.

### Act I: Before the Shot

1765–1775. Stamp Act unrest, committees of correspondence, Boston under pressure, smuggling, informants. You build legend, allies, and public will while the Crown still thinks it owns the morning.

### Act II: The Break

1775–1777. Lexington energy, siege of Boston, Declaration momentum. Your pre-war choices decide how the war ignites. Make it early, late, louder, or bloodier.

### Act III: Forging a Nation

1777–1783. Valley Forge grit, turning points, Yorktown payoff. The smudges get bolder. History remembers the cause at its bravest because you made it so.
